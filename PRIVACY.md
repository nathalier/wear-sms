# Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Wear SMS is an open source SMS app for Wear OS watches with their own SIM.

## What the app accesses

- **SMS messages:** the app reads, writes and deletes messages in the watch's system SMS database so you can see and send them.
- **Contacts:** the app reads your contacts to search for people and to show their names instead of phone numbers.
- **Notifications:** the app posts notifications when a message arrives.

## What the app does with it

- Messages are sent and received through your mobile carrier as normal SMS, and your carrier's rates and policies apply.
- The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data to any server, the developer or anyone else.
- The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
- Nothing is collected, stored or shared outside your watch.

## Export

If you choose "Export all", the app writes your messages to a JSON file in the `Download/wear-sms` folder on the watch.
The file stays on the watch until you move or delete it yourself.
